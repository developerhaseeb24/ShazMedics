About Dr Syed Nawaz Ali

Dr. Syed N Ali is a General Practitioner and Independent Medical Expert with an extensive experience in healthcare, medico-legal reporting and clinical governance.
He is fully registered with the General Medical Council (GMC) and is accredited by MedCo as an Independent Medical Expert, Dr Ali is committed to delivering a professional, impartial and evidence-based medical service.
He provides GP consultations and independent medico-legal assessments, offering a thorough objective clinical evaluation and court-compliant reports for personal injury claims. His areas of expertise include soft tissue injuries, whiplash and non-whiplash injuries, in addition to associated psychological symptoms.
Dr Ali combines clinical expertise with meticulous, patient-focused approach, ensuring that every assessment is conducted with professionalism, accuracy and care. He upholds the highest standards of patient safety, confidentiality and ethical practice, with a strong commitment to clinical excellence and professional integrity.
